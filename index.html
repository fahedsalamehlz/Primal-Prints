Good to know

How long until it ships?

Everything is printed to order. Most orders ship within 3–5 business days by USPS, with tracking. Custom orders get a timeline with the quote.

How much is shipping?

Flat $7 shipping per order, no matter how many items you get — we pack them together.

Is checkout secure?

Payments go through PayPal's own secure checkout. We never see or store your card number — you can pay with PayPal, Venmo, or a debit or credit card without a PayPal account.

What if something isn't right?

Message us and we'll make it right. Personalized and custom items can't be returned unless they arrive damaged or wrong, in which case we'll reprint them.

Do you do department or bulk orders?

Yes — use the custom order form with the quantity and any agency text, and we'll quote volume pricing.

Can I still buy on Etsy?

Yes, our Etsy shop has the same items. Buying here costs a little less because it skips the marketplace fees.
